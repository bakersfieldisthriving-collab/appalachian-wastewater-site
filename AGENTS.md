# Appalachian Wastewater — Project Rules

## Brand Positioning

Appalachian Wastewater is a modern wastewater and septic services company serving East Tennessee.

The brand should feel:

* professional
* modern
* trustworthy
* regional
* responsive
* premium but approachable

Avoid:

* cartoon septic branding
* goofy plumbing language
* overly corporate utility language
* exaggerated industrial jargon
* cheesy blue-collar stereotypes

The company serves:

* homeowners
* rural property owners
* restaurants
* campgrounds
* cabins
* churches
* rental properties
* small businesses
* light commercial properties

Use broad terms like:

* properties
* East Tennessee properties
* homes and businesses
* property owners
* septic and wastewater systems

Avoid repeatedly saying only “homes.”

---

## Visual Design Rules

### Style

* modern wastewater authority
* clean infrastructure aesthetic
* minimal and professional
* dark premium design
* utility-inspired visual structure

### Primary Colors

* deep navy
* charcoal
* slate gray
* steel blue

### Accent Colors

* reflective silver
* minimal burnt copper
* minimal safety orange

Avoid:

* bright plumber blues
* neon colors
* muddy greens
* cartoon gradients

---

## Typography

Use clean modern sans-serif typography.

Preferred fonts:

* Inter
* Montserrat
* Sora

Use bold typography sparingly and professionally.

---

## Website Goals

The website should:

* build trust quickly
* feel premium
* convert service leads
* rank well locally
* feel approachable
* communicate professionalism

The website should NOT feel:

* like a municipal engineering contractor
* like a generic plumbing website
* like a cheap local handyman company

## Technical Rules

* Maintain Astro architecture
* Maintain reusable components
* Keep layouts modular
* Optimize for Vercel deployment
* Keep performance high
* Preserve responsive/mobile-first behavior

---

## Content Rules

Tone should be:

* clear
* confident
* professional
* approachable

Avoid:

* excessive technical jargon
* excessive corporate wording
* fake urgency
* cheesy marketing phrases

Keep copy easy for normal property owners to understand while preserving authority.
